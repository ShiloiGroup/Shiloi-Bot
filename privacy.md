**Last updated: November 17, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Shiloï Protect** collects, processes, stores, and uses information when you interact with the bot or use it on a Discord server.

By using Shiloï Protect, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

Shiloï Protect may collect and process the following categories of information, depending on the features enabled on a server:

### Discord Information
- Discord User IDs.
- Discord Server IDs.
- Discord Channel IDs.
- Discord Role IDs.
- Usernames and display names, when necessary.

### Moderation Information
- Warnings and moderation records.
- Ban, kick, mute, and timeout records.
- Moderation actions performed by server staff.
- Information related to security incidents, spam, or suspicious activity.

### Server Configuration
- Bot settings and preferences.
- Moderation and protection configurations.
- Logging channel settings.
- Custom server-specific options.

### Technical Information
- Error reports and diagnostic information.
- Information required to maintain the bot's functionality.
- Other technical data necessary to provide the service.

The exact information processed depends on the features enabled and how each server is configured.

## 2. How We Use Information

Collected information may be used to:
- Provide the bot's moderation and security features.
- Detect and prevent spam, raids, and abusive behavior.
- Store moderation records and server configurations.
- Generate logs and notifications.
- Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the service.
- Protect the bot, its infrastructure, and its users.

We do not intend to use collected information for purposes unrelated to the operation of Shiloï Protect.

## 3. Data Storage and Security

Information may be stored in databases and other systems required for the bot to operate.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect stored information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no online service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

Shiloï Protect may rely on third-party services to operate, such as:
- Discord, for communication and bot functionality.
- Hosting providers, to run the bot and store its data.
- External services required for specific integrations or features.

Information may be processed by these providers when necessary to deliver the service.

We do not sell users' personal information.

Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

## 5. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the features used.

Where appropriate, information may be deleted or anonymized when it is no longer required.

## 6. User Rights and Data Requests

Depending on applicable data protection laws, you may have the right to:
- Request access to your personal information.
- Request correction of inaccurate information.
- Request deletion of your personal information.
- Object to or request restriction of certain processing activities.
- Request information about how your data is used.

To submit a privacy-related request, please contact the Shiloï Protect team through its official Discord server.

We may need to verify your identity before processing a request.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Shiloï Protect is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

Users must comply with Discord's minimum age requirements and applicable laws when using Discord and its services.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to Shiloï Protect, its features, its data practices, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updated version will be published on the official Privacy Policy page.

Continued use of the bot after changes are published constitutes acceptance of the updated policy, where legally permitted.

## 9. Contact

For questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the processing of personal information, please contact the Shiloï Protect team through its official Discord server.
